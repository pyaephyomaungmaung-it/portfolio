## 6. How to Enable "Open to Work" on LinkedIn

### Step-by-step Instructions:
1. Go to your LinkedIn profile page.
2. Click the **Open to** button below your profile photo and headline.
3. Select **Finding a new job**.
4. Fill in your preferred job preferences (see recommended settings below).
5. Choose who can see that you are open to work:
   - **Recruiters only:** Only people using LinkedIn Recruiter see your status (keeps your current network unaware; no green photo frame).
   - **All LinkedIn members:** Adds the green **#OpenToWork** badge to your profile picture and posts an update to your feed (maximizes visibility).
6. Click **Add to profile**.

### Recommended Preferences to Fill In:
* **Job Titles (Add up to 5):**
  - `Software Engineer`
  - `Full Stack Engineer`
  - `AI Engineer`
  - `Python Developer`
  - `Junior Software Engineer`
* **Location:** `Singapore` (or specific target regions)
* **Location Types:** Select `On-site`, `Hybrid`, and `Remote`
* **Start Date:** `Immediately, I'm actively applying`
* **Employment Types:** `Full-time`, `Contract`, `Internship`
